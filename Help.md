### The following are the control option available for you:
|S.N.|Button     | Action                                     |
|----|-----------|--------------------------------------------|
|1.  | R         |Restore the initial view                    |  
|2.  | W,A,S,D   |Forward,Backward,Left and Right                    |  
|3.  | ↑         |Move Camera Upward                    |  
|4.  | ↓         |Move Camera Downward                    |
|5.  |  →←     | Rotate Left and Right | 
|6.  | ↓         |Move Camera Downward                    |
